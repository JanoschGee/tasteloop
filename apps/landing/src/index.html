Tasteloop collects your team's taste (every reference, every “why”) and makes it readable for Claude and your agents.

Get it free why: because the last 20% is yours.

How it works

  1. Circle it.{{CIRCLE:step1:150:70}}

    Drop a screenshot into your team chat. An ad, a photo, a colour, a line of copy.

  2. Say why.

    Add a voice note or a caption. why: the light, not the model.

    It lands in a shared memory, with the image and the reason.

  3. Your agents get it.

    Every week, Tasteloop proposes rules for your brand guide. Approved rules become files your agents read, with the references as proof.

Built for teams, not for one person's taste

  • Everyone adds.

    Each contributor gets their own pen. You see who backs a rule: “3 people, 5 references”.

  • Disagreements surface.

    If one person loves what another dislikes, Tasteloop shows both sides. Nothing contested quietly becomes a rule.

  • Humans approve.

    Agents suggest. People decide. The core of your brand is locked and never changes on its own.

Our promise

  • Your taste stays yours. Plain Markdown and images in your own Obsidian vault. No lock-in, nothing to export.
  • Runs on your machine. Your laptop, a Mac mini or a small server. Only the AI calls leave it.
  • Made for the last 20%. We like AI. We just think taste is a team sport. No slop.
