  2. 02 · Classification

    Claude reads the image and the why.

    The voice note is transcribed on your machine. Claude analyses the image together with your reason: what you like, what you don't, the style, how it fits the brand.

    It lands as a plain Markdown note with the image in your Obsidian vault.

  3. 03 · Brand memory

    The guide learns. The core stays locked.

    Every week, Tasteloop proposes changes to your brand guide, with the evidence images and who backs it. Disagreements show up next to it.

    A person approves. Only then is it written, and only into the part that may learn. Campaign uploads become a brief right away.

For teams

Built for teams, not for one person's taste

Anyone on the team can add a reference or a reason. You see who backs a rule, and who doesn't.

  • Everyone adds.

    Every note carries a name. You see who backs a rule: “3 people, 5 references”.

  • Disagreements surface.

    If one person loves what another dislikes, Tasteloop shows both sides. Nothing contested quietly becomes a rule.

  • Humans approve.

    Agents suggest. People decide. The core of your brand is locked and never changes on its own.
