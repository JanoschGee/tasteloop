The short version

This website is a static page with one sign-up form. It has no accounts, no cookies, no tracking and no third-party fonts or scripts. We only collect personal data when you fill in the form (see “Sign-up form and e-mails”). Otherwise the only data processed when you visit is what any web server needs to deliver a page.

Hosting and server logs

The website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. When you open a page, Vercel automatically processes technical data in server logs: your IP address, date and time of the request, the requested page, the referring page, your browser and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website securely and to protect it against misuse.

Legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We have concluded a data processing agreement with Vercel. Vercel may process data in the USA. Vercel is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; in addition, the EU Standard Contractual Clauses apply. Logs are deleted by Vercel according to its retention periods. More: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

No cookies, no analytics

We do not set cookies and we do not use analytics or advertising tools. Fonts are served from our own domain.

Sign-up form and e-mails

To get the download link you fill in a form. We collect: your e-mail address, your answer to the question in the form, whether you want occasional updates, the time of your sign-up and whether and when you confirmed it. After you submit the form you receive one e-mail with a confirmation link (double opt-in). Only after you confirm do we send you the link.

Purposes and legal bases: we use your e-mail address to send you the download link you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If you tick the box for updates, we also send you occasional news about Tasteloop, based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time through the unsubscribe link in every e-mail or by writing to us; this does not affect processing before the withdrawal. We read your answer to understand what teams need, based on our legitimate interest in improving Tasteloop (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this at any time.

Processor: we use Brevo to store the sign-ups and send the e-mails (Sendinblue SAS, 106 boulevard Haussmann, 75008 Paris, France). The data is stored in the EU. We have concluded a data processing agreement with Brevo.

Retention: we keep your data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Sign-ups that are not confirmed are deleted after 30 days. To protect the form against misuse, the number of requests per IP address is limited for a few minutes in memory; this is not stored.

Links to other websites

After you confirm your e-mail, the welcome page links to GitHub, where the Tasteloop source code is published. If you follow the link, GitHub's own privacy policy applies. No data is sent to GitHub before you click.

Tasteloop software

Tasteloop is open-source software that you run on your own machine with your own accounts and API keys. Data you process with it (images, voice notes, notes, brand guides) stays with you and the services you choose to connect (for example Telegram, Anthropic, Obsidian). We do not receive or process any of it.

To exercise them, contact us at the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW).
